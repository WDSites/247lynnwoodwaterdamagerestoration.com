Privacy Policy

Plain-language notes on calls, recordings and website data.

This policy explains what happens when you visit 247lynnwoodwaterdamagerestoration.com or call the number listed on it. 247 Lynnwood Water Damage Restoration is a call-based service for water damage and mold problems in Lynnwood, Washington and nearby towns. There are no forms, accounts or chat boxes on this site, so most of what we handle arrives by telephone.

Calls to our line

When you call, we may ask for your name, the address where the damage is, a callback number and a short description of what happened. We use that information to understand the problem, explain the scope and price before work starts, and send licensed and insured restoration professionals to the right address.

Calls to our line may be recorded for quality and training purposes.

If you do not want to share a detail, tell the dispatcher. Some details, such as the address, are needed before anyone can be sent to a job.

What this website collects

This site has no forms, so it does not collect information you type into it. Like most websites, the hosting service may keep standard server logs, such as the pages requested, the date and the type of browser, to keep the site running and secure.

Phone numbers on this site are standard tel: links. Tapping one opens your phone dialer. Nothing is sent until you place the call.

Third-party content

Pages carry an embedded Google map and load fonts from Google Fonts. Those services receive the request your browser makes to load them and are governed by their own privacy policies. We do not control what they collect.

How information is used and kept

Information from a call is used to respond to your request, to schedule and document work, to answer your questions afterward and to review call quality. It is kept only as long as needed for those purposes or as the law requires.

We limit access to people who need the information to do their jobs. No method of storage or transmission is perfectly secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

Your choices

You can ask us what we have on file about your call, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it where the law allows. To do that, email contact@247lynnwoodwaterdamagerestoration.com and include the phone number you called from. Water emergencies should go to the phone line, not email.

Children

This site is for adults who own or manage property. It is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will appear on this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Coverage area

Our Lynnwood service area

Dispatch covers Lynnwood, Edmonds, Mountlake Terrace, Mukilteo, Bothell and Everett.
