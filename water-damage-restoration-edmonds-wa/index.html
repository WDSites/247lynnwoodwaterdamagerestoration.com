Downtown, the ferry dock and the marsh edge

The 98020 side of town holds City Hall, the ferry terminal at 199 Sunset Ave S and the old downtown grid. The city's shoreline program says more than 90 percent of the Edmonds shoreline is armored by the BNSF railroad bed and bulkheads. Inland, the same program studies the Edmonds Marsh for its role in the flooding problem at the Dayton Street and State Route 104 intersection. A shop or home near that low point that takes in water needs emergency extraction first, then moisture mapping of slab edges and walls with a thermal imaging camera and a moisture meter.

Perrinville, Highway 99 and Westgate: creek country and ditch country

The plan also describes glacial till, perched groundwater and streets that rely on ditches and culverts. A plugged culvert can push water against a foundation in a single storm, so clear grates only when it is safe to stand outside.

The city runs neighborhood planning for Highway 99, Westgate and Five Corners. Older apartment blocks along those corridors share floors and walls, so one failed supply line can soak a unit below. Emergency water removal comes first there, and then the shared ceilings and wall cavities get dried.

Houses built between 1950 and 1969

Edmonds' housing element says much of the existing stock dates from 1950 to 1969, and roughly 63.5 percent of homes were single-family in 2010. Houses that age often carry original drain lines, older water heaters and crawl spaces that hold humidity.

When water is spreading in Edmonds

A burst line, a stalled grinder pump or sewage in a floor drain is an emergency, and wet materials start to fail quickly. Call the 24/7 line from a dry spot and tell the dispatcher what is wet, where it is coming from and whether it looks clear or dirty. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock, so a Saturday night flood gets the same attention as a weekday one.

Electrical panel, gas and sewage: stay out first

If water is touching the electrical panel, outlets or a gas appliance, or you smell gas, leave the house and call 911 or your electric or gas utility before you call us. Never wade into a flooded basement while the power is on. Sewage carries bacteria, so keep children and pets away and wait for crews in protective gear.

Before we arrive: four moves that limit the damage

Close the valve

Turn the main valve clockwise until it stops, then open the lowest faucet to confirm the line has drained. For a fixture leak, the shutoff under the sink or behind the toilet is enough.

Cut power with care

Switch off breakers to wet rooms only when the panel is dry and you can reach it without touching water. If either condition fails, leave it alone and call the utility.

Lift what you can

Put electronics, papers, shoes and small furniture on counters or beds. Foil or plastic under table legs keeps stain and moisture off wet carpet.

Keep clear, then call

Keep people and pets out of dirty water. Then call the 24/7 line and describe what you see, because that guides which equipment goes on the truck.

City water, sewer and the Edmonds system

Edmonds buys drinking water from the Alderwood Water and Wastewater District, which gets it from the City of Everett, and then moves it through 138 miles of mains and 18 pressure reducing stations. A sudden pressure change is one reason a fitting that held for decades can let go. Call the city for the main, and call us for what is inside your walls.

The sewer side runs over 186 miles of main, 14 pump stations and 26 residential grinder pumps, ending at the plant at 200 2nd Ave S. A grinder pump that stops can send wastewater back into a home, so treat a high-level alarm as an emergency. The same plant treats flow from Mountlake Terrace, which we also serve, and our crews work the shoreline up to Mukilteo as well.

What we do in Edmonds, in the order most calls need it

Most calls begin with extraction units pulling standing water from basements, garages and crawl spaces, then water mitigation with air movers, LGR dehumidifiers and moisture meters to dry framing and subfloor over the following days. Ask any company whether its crews work to the ANSI/IICRC S500 standard for water damage restoration.

Damp materials left wet for several days invite mold. When we find it, mold remediation follows the ANSI/IICRC S520 standard, and health questions belong with your physician. Once everything is dry, water damage repair covers the drywall, flooring and trim.

Cost factors, insurance and permits

Price in Edmonds follows how wet the structure got and for how long: the water category, the area affected, the materials involved, how many air movers and dehumidifiers run and for how many days, and how much rebuild is left afterward. The 24/7 line can walk through those drivers for your own house.

Insurers commonly treat a sudden pipe burst differently from seepage that built up over months, and flood from outside is usually a separate policy. Read yours first. Photos, daily moisture logs and a written scope from the crew give you paper to hand your insurer. Rebuild work may need a permit, so ask the Edmonds Permit Center at City Hall, 121 5th Ave N.

Questions we hear

Do you cover every ZIP code in Edmonds?

Yes. Edmonds has two ZIP codes, 98026 and 98020, and we answer emergency calls from both. 98020 holds downtown, City Hall and the ferry terminal on Sunset Ave S, and the directory also lists Woodway as an alias. 98026 is the larger ZIP by 2020 Census count, with 38,610 residents.

Is emergency water removal available in Edmonds?

Yes. Emergency dispatch is available around the clock, including nights, weekends and holidays.

What should I do if a grinder pump alarm sounds or sewage backs up?

Stop using water, keep people and pets away from the affected area, and call the city's after-hours utility line to find out whether the problem is in the main or on your side. Then call our 24/7 line for extraction and sanitizing of the affected area.

My yard near Perrinville Creek stays soggy. Does that mean water damage inside?

Not always, but wet ground against a foundation can wick moisture into slabs, crawl spaces and wall bases. A moisture meter reading inside settles it, and drainage work is a separate trade.

Do I need a permit for repairs after a leak in Edmonds?

It depends on the scope. Drying equipment does not need one, but rebuilding walls, rewiring or replumbing might. The Edmonds Permit Center takes building permit applications through mybuildingpermit.com, so ask them before work starts.
