Contact 247 Lynnwood Water Damage Restoration

The phone line is answered 24 hours a day, every day. This page lists what to have ready when you call.

Contact for 247 Lynnwood Water Damage Restoration starts with a phone call. There are no forms on this site because water damage does not wait for a reply email. Call the 24/7 line, tell the dispatcher what happened, and the next step gets explained before any work begins.

What to have ready when you call

A short, specific description helps the dispatcher sort your call quickly. You do not need the right trade terms. "The ceiling under the upstairs bathroom is dripping" is enough.

  • The street address, including the city, so the right crew and the right ZIP code are used.
  • What happened and when you noticed it, such as a burst pipe, a washing machine hose, a roof leak or a backed-up drain.
  • Whether the water source is stopped. If it is not, shut off the main water valve first if that is safe.
  • Which rooms and floors are wet, and whether water reached ceilings, walls, carpet, hardwood or a basement.
  • Whether the water looks clean, cloudy or contaminated, for example sewage or floodwater.
  • Your insurance company name if you have it handy. You do not need a claim number to call.

Emergency or planned work

If water is spreading or sewage is involved, treat it as an emergency and call right away. The line is the same for planned jobs such as a mold question, a ceiling stain that is not growing, or repairs after drying is complete. Same-day response is offered when available, and the dispatcher explains scope and price before work starts.

If water has reached a panel, outlet or gas appliance, stay out of that area and call your electric or gas utility or 911 first. Then call us.

Where we serve

The office is in Lynnwood, and calls cover Lynnwood and nearby towns. The pages for Edmonds, Mountlake Terrace, Mukilteo, Bothell and Everett list the ZIP codes covered in each place. The service area page pulls it all together.

The street address is for mail and records. Crews are dispatched to your property, so there is no need to visit the office during an emergency.

Questions we hear

Is the phone line really open at night and on weekends?

Yes. The emergency line is answered 24 hours a day, every day of the year, including holidays.

Response depends on the situation, and a same-day response is offered when available. The dispatcher will tell you honestly what to expect on your call.

Can I send photos or fill out a form instead of calling?

This site has no forms. A phone call gets a real person asking the right questions right away, and you can describe photos you have taken. Keep your photos, because they help later if you file an insurance claim.

Do I need an insurance claim number to call?

No. Call first and get the water stopped, extracted and contained. Claim details can come later, and your insurer will tell you what it needs.
